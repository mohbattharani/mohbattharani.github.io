I am an AI Researcher and Developer at [TWG Global](https://www.twgglobal.com), where I design and deploy Large and Small Language Models for enterprise financial AI applications — including loan tape analysis, financial document understanding, and RAG/GraphRAG/multi-agent systems in production. I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science from [Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute](https://www.rpi.edu),, advised by [Prof. Mohammad J. Zaki](https://www.cs.rpi.edu/~zaki/), where my research focused on improving Large Language Models (LLMs) and Multi-Modal Models (MMMs) by integrating Knowledge Graphs (KGs) through Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), with applications in food computing, document understanding, and text generation. I have also worked with researchers at IBM Research and Nokia Bell Labs on visually rich document understanding, bias in multi-modal LLMs, and agentic AI for fault analysis.
